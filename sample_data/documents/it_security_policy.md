# Meridian Labs IT Security Policy

# Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source control and the VPN. Passwords must not be reused across company systems. Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts.

# Devices

All company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the security team within 1 hour.

# Incident Reporting

Security incidents must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery. Critical incidents trigger an on-call response within 15 minutes. The security team publishes a written post-incident review within 5 working days.

# Data Classification

Company data has four classes: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data must never be stored on personal devices. Confidential data must be encrypted when sent outside the company.

# Backups

Production databases are backed up every night. Backups are retained for 35 days. A restore test is carried out once every quarter.

# Network Access

The VPN is required for all access to internal systems from outside the office. Guest Wi-Fi is separated from the corporate network.
