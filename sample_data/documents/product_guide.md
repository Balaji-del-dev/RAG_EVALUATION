# Atlas Edge Router Product Guide

The Atlas Edge Router is the main hardware product of Meridian Labs, a fictional company used as sample data.

# Warranty

The Atlas Edge Router has a 3-year limited hardware warranty. The warranty does not cover damage caused by water or by unauthorized repairs. Replacement units are shipped within 5 business days of an approved claim.

# Technical Specifications

The router has 4 Ethernet ports and supports speeds of up to 2.5 Gbps per port. Maximum power consumption is 18 watts. The operating temperature range is 0 to 45 degrees Celsius. Firmware updates are released every 6 weeks.

# Service Plans

The Standard plan includes email support during business hours with a response time of 8 hours. The Premium plan includes 24x7 phone support with a response time of 1 hour. The Premium plan has an uptime commitment of 99.9 percent per month.

# Management API

The management API allows 600 requests per minute for each account. Requests above this limit receive an HTTP 429 response. API tokens expire after 90 days and must be renewed in the admin console.
