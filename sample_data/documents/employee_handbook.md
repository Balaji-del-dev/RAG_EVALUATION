# Meridian Labs Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to all full-time employees of Meridian Labs, a fictional company used as sample data.

# Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive days need approval from a manager at least 2 weeks in advance.

# Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

# Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption of the child.

# Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing their probation period. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST, and employees must be reachable during these hours. Working from outside India for more than 14 days requires written approval from Human Resources.

# Probation and Notice

The probation period for new employees is 6 months. During probation, the notice period is 15 days. After probation, the notice period is 60 days.

# Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase. The meal allowance during business travel is INR 1,500 per day. Any single expense above INR 25,000 requires pre-approval from a department head. Economy class is the standard for all flights shorter than 8 hours.

# Learning Budget

Each employee has a learning budget of INR 40,000 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year.
